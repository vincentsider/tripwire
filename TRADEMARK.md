# Trademark & brand policy

> **Plain-language summary:** the *code* in this repository is free and open
> (Apache-2.0) — fork it, run it, build on it. The project *name* and the
> *"verified" badge* are **not** free: they are DeepBlocker's brand, and only
> badges issued by DeepBlocker's own service are official. This is the same split
> every open-source project with a protected brand uses (e.g. the Linux Mark: the
> code is open, the name is a trademark). It is what lets us give the code away
> while keeping the trust behind the badge meaningful.

> **Status note:** the project name is currently under review for a potential
> change (to avoid confusion with an unrelated, established security product).
> Once finalized, this document and the repository will be updated to the new
> name. "The name" below refers to whichever name the project ships under.

## 1. What the open-source license does and does not grant

The source code is licensed under **Apache-2.0**. Under **section 6** of that
license, an open-source copyright/patent license grants **no trademark rights**.
So the code license lets you use the *software*; it does **not** let you use our
*name, logo, or the "verified" badge* as if you were us.

## 2. DeepBlocker's marks

The following are trademarks of DeepBlocker (whether or not registered):

- the **project/product name** and any associated logo;
- the **"verified" trust-badge** design and the word marks that appear on it
  (e.g. "verified", "tools verified", "tools audited").

## 3. The badge is a certification-style mark

The badge signifies that **DeepBlocker's own service** verified a site's WebMCP
tool surface and continues to re-check it live. Accordingly:

- **Only a badge issued and signed by DeepBlocker's service is official.** A
  self-hosted or forked deployment can issue its **own** badges under its **own**
  name, but must **not** present them as DeepBlocker/official badges, and must not
  use our name or badge design to imply we verified or endorsed anything.
- A signed badge is **revocable**. DeepBlocker may revoke a badge (e.g. when a
  site's tools change or ownership proof lapses); revocation is part of what the
  mark means.

## 4. What you MAY do

- Use, modify, fork, and redistribute the **code** under Apache-2.0.
- State factually that your product "uses" or "is built on" the open-source
  project, or is "compatible with" it — without using our logo or implying
  endorsement.
- Run your own instance for your own testing.

## 5. What you MAY NOT do without written permission

- Use the project name, logo, or badge design as the name/branding of your own
  product or service, or in a domain name, in a way likely to cause confusion.
- Present a self-issued or forged badge as an official DeepBlocker verification.
- Imply sponsorship, affiliation, or endorsement by DeepBlocker.

## 6. Questions / permission

For any brand or badge use beyond the above, contact DeepBlocker via
https://deepblocker.ai.
