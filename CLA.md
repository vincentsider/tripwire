# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

> **Why this exists (plain language):** to keep the project healthy and to keep
> DeepBlocker's options open (for example, to offer a commercially-licensed or
> dual-licensed edition later), the project needs clear rights over every
> contribution. This CLA does **two** things: (1) you keep ownership of your
> contribution, and (2) you grant DeepBlocker broad rights to use and re-license
> it. Without this, a project can become **permanently unable to relicense** —
> the Linux kernel, which took contributions from thousands of people with no
> CLA, famously cannot change its license. We are avoiding that trap early.
>
> **This is a real legal agreement. It has not yet been reviewed by counsel;
> DeepBlocker will finalize the exact wording. Do not rely on this draft as
> final.**

By submitting a contribution (a pull request, patch, or any code, documentation,
or other material) to this project, You agree to the following, for that and all
future contributions to the project:

## 1. Definitions

"You" means the individual or legal entity making a contribution. "Contribution"
means any original work of authorship You intentionally submit to the project.

## 2. You keep ownership

You retain all right, title, and interest in Your Contribution. This agreement
does **not** transfer ownership; it grants licenses.

## 3. Copyright license

You grant DeepBlocker and recipients of software distributed by DeepBlocker a
perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, irrevocable copyright license
to reproduce, prepare derivative works of, publicly display, publicly perform,
sublicense, **and re-license** Your Contribution and such derivative works,
**under any license terms, including both open-source and proprietary/commercial
licenses.** This is the clause that preserves DeepBlocker's ability to offer the
project under different license terms in the future.

## 4. Patent license

You grant DeepBlocker and recipients a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive,
royalty-free, irrevocable (except as stated below) patent license to make, use,
sell, offer to sell, import, and otherwise transfer Your Contribution, where such
license applies to patent claims You can license that are necessarily infringed
by Your Contribution alone or by combination with the project. If any entity
institutes patent litigation alleging that the project or a Contribution
constitutes patent infringement, the patent licenses granted here to that entity
terminate.

## 5. You have the right to grant this

You represent that: You are legally entitled to grant the above licenses; each
Contribution is Your original creation (or You have the necessary rights to
submit it); and if Your employer has rights to intellectual property You create,
You have received permission to make the Contributions on behalf of that
employer, or the employer has waived such rights.

## 6. No obligation / no warranty

You are not expected to provide support for Your Contribution. Unless required by
law or agreed in writing, You provide Your Contribution "AS IS", without
warranties or conditions of any kind.

## 7. Notice of non-original material

If You wish to submit work that is not Your original creation, You may submit it
separately, clearly identifying its source and any license or restriction.

---

## How to sign

Until an automated CLA check (a "CLA bot") is enabled on pull requests, indicate
your agreement by adding a `Signed-off-by` line to each commit (Developer
Certificate of Origin style), which also records provenance:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <your.email@example.com>
```

and, on your first pull request, comment:

> I have read the CLA and I agree to it on behalf of myself (and, if applicable,
> my employer).

Entities (companies) contributing on behalf of employees should contact
DeepBlocker via https://deepblocker.ai to arrange a corporate CLA.
